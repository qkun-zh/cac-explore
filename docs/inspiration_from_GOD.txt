## §8 The Channel-Floor Program v2 [math rewritten after CPU verification; every step labeled DERIVED / FITTED / ASSUMED]

### §8.0 Verification ledger for the previous version
| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Constant-error floor at small N (channel term) | **CONFIRMED** | F1 flat region, σ₀≈2 |
| Linear-in-N error growth in tail (prior/saturation term) | **CONFIRMED** | F1 slope 0.686, AIC-selected |
| Errors are zero-mean variance | **REFUTED — they are systematic bias** | F2: median signed = −|err| |
| Measurement improves monotonically with token-grid density | **REFUTED** | F4 U-shape, min at training res |
| Tail information floor estimable from existing sweep | **WITHDRAWN** | degenerate fit (m∞<0), n=17 |
| Per-object mass falls with scene-level crowding | **INCONCLUSIVE by design flaw** (size confound) | P4 uncontrolled |

### §8.2 DERIVED: the channel floor (explains σ₀)
Scene μ = Σ m̄ δ_{xᵢ}; observation y = h∗μ + ε, ε∼N(0,σ²I). For any linear statistic T(y)=∫φ̂(−ω)y(ω)dω,
E[T] = m̄ ∫ φ̂(−ω)ĥ(ω) S(ω) dω, S(ω)=Σᵢe^{−iω·xᵢ}.
S(ω) is configuration-dependent for all ω≠0 (Poisson fluctuations ~√N); only S(0)=N is configuration-free. Hence position-robust count information lives at DC, φ̂ concentrated at ω=0, giving
Var(T) = σ²∫|φ̂|² ⪆ σ²/(m̄²|ĥ(0)|²)   ⇒   an N-independent absolute-error floor. **Matches F1's σ₀≈2.**
Caveat (ASSUMED): h linear and DC-preserving. D1 tests DC preservation; if ĥ(0)≈0 the floor must be re-derived from higher-frequency statistics with √N-growing S(ω) variance (constant *relative* error instead).

### §8.3 DERIVED + FITTED: visibility attenuation (explains the 0.69 slope as BIAS, not variance)
Model (ASSUMED): each object contributes its *visible* footprint; readout integrates evidence linearly with perfect mean-mass calibration. For planar opaque overlap with Poisson germs at coverage f, the expected visible fraction of a disk is
v(f) = (1/πr²)∫_disk exp(−λ|B(u,r)|) du ≈ e^{−λπr²} = 1 − f   (interior approximation, boundary correction O(r/√A)).
Then E[N̂] ≈ N·v̄(f), i.e., signed error = −N(1−v̄): a *systematic multiplicative undercount*, exactly the bias shape F2 reports (not a variance term — a variance term would give symmetric errors).
FITTED: implied visibility v̄ = 1 − (median signed err)/N gives ≈0.67 on [200,500) and ≈0.40 on [500,∞) — physically plausible for geometric coverage f∈[0.3,0.6], decreasing as density rises, with an extra collapse at N≳800 (F3, n=8, fragile). The match is order-correct, NOT a precision test (confounds: category mix, box sizes, halo softness).
Consequence: because the deficit is a smooth *gain* error conditioned on local state, global recalibration is mathematically incapable of fixing it (one scalar cannot track a state-dependent gain) — this retroactively derives the old H0033/H0034 failures and prescribes the cure: condition the gain on measurable state (seed 2 / experiment O1).

### §8.4 NEW DERIVED FACT: resolution U-shape (three-term decomposition)
Write bucket error at eval size S as
MAE_b(S) ≈ Floor_b + Shift_b(|ln(S/S₀)|) + SatGain_b(S),
where SatGain_b decreases in S (finer grid → higher v(f) evidence), Shift_b grows with distance from the training resolution S₀=392 (positional-embedding interpolation, patch-to-object ratio drift — a train/eval distribution shift), Floor_b is S-independent (§8.2). Sparse buckets: f≈0 ⟹ SatGain inactive ⟹ pure U from Shift. Dense buckets: SatGain dominates ⟹ monotone decrease. **This reproduces all five buckets' shapes in F4 qualitatively and is falsifiable per-bucket.**
Consequences: (a) any-resolution routing may only move *dense-side* images up; (b) the tail information floor CANNOT be extrapolated from this sweep (Shift contaminates large-S points for sparse buckets, and dense buckets have not saturated: [500,∞) fit degenerate) — only R0-I3's synthetic arm can locate it.

### §8.5 Assembled law (all terms now empirically anchored)
|err| ≈ σ₀ + β·(N−N*)₊ , σ₀≈2 (DERIVED §8.2 + FITTED), β≈0.69 = 1−v̄ (DERIVED form §8.3 + FITTED magnitude), N*≈25–50 (empirical).
Open: whether β flattens (floor reached) or accelerates (u(f) onset) beyond N≈800 — underpowered (n=8); decidable by R0-I3.

### §8.6 Feasibility of ≤10 (updated)
Tail ≤25% relative error requires v̄≳0.75 sustained through N≈1000 AND exemplar-conditioned m̄ with CV≲15%. v̄≈0.30 measured at the extreme ⟹ **currently failing killer-1 by 2.5×**; no physical violation — but the burden is on raising effective visibility (bandwidth/amodal evidence), not on recalibration.

### §8.7 Standing experiments
Unchanged: R0-I1 (scalar-vs-map probe, GPU 10min), R0-I2 (three-tier localization + D1 saturation probe), R0-I3 (synthetic floor — now the *only* route to the tail information limit, upgraded priority). New: **O1 offline conditional-gain fit** (server CPU, minutes): split-half fit of N̂=g(box_area, N_hat)·Σρ on existing dumps; measures how much of the 0.69 linear term is reclaimable by state-conditioned calibration without any training.

### §8.8 Endgame (unchanged in substance, re-derived basis)
Amortized Bayesian counter with proper-scoring posterior output (immune to gain collapse by construction), exemplar-carried m̄ calibration (killer 2), bandwidth widening via multi-resolution *dense-side-only* evidence (killer 1), feature-saturation repair via partial FT if D1 confirms closure (killer 3). MAE converges to the posterior median the channel permits — the operational definition of the information-theoretic limit.
