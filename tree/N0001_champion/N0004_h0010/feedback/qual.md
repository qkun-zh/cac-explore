# QUAL — N0004_h0010 (H0010 per-exemplar gate + H0009 channel gate, co-tested)

## 1. How the two gates compose, in model.py terms

Exemplar tokens flow `e = self.exemplar(...)` (model.py:172), shape (B, K, 256). Then TWO multiplicative gates, in forward order:

1. **Per-exemplar** (H0010): `eg = torch.sigmoid(self.exemplar_gate(e)); e = e * eg.unsqueeze(-1)` (model.py:173–175). One scalar per exemplar template from a `Linear(256→1)` (model.py:163) with zero-init weight + bias 3.0 (model.py:164–165) ⇒ starts at sigmoid(3) ≈ 0.953 per template, and **can only shrink** (sigmoid ∈ (0,1)).
2. **Image-global per-channel** (H0009): `gate = torch.sigmoid(self.channel_gate(fine.mean(dim=(2,3)))); e = e * gate.unsqueeze(1)` (model.py:176–178). One (B,256) vector from `Linear(128→256)` (model.py:160), default init ⇒ ≈0.5 at step 0, shared across **all K** templates.

Composition is a rank-1 multiplicative factor per image: `E' = E ⊙ (eg_k ⊗ g_c)`. Axes are distinct (K vs 256), so order is algebraically irrelevant; the meaningful structure is that per-template sensitivity is only ever a single scalar, re-shaped by the same channel profile for every template.

Concrete geometry effects at step 0: every key/value entry of `e` sits at ≈ 0.953 × 0.5 ≈ **0.48× parent magnitude**. The Condenser (model.py:111–125) computes attention logits `q·k/√d` with `q = proj_in(fine patches)`, `k/v = e` (model.py:122–123): halved keys halve all logits → a uniformly softer/less peaky attention distribution, and halved values → the aggregated attended signal `a` enters `q = norm1(tok + a)` at ~4× smaller contribution vs the untouched path. `e_mean` is computed **after both gates** (model.py:181) and feeds the GCA aux (model.py:228), so the count-aux sees the same 0.48× init scaling; the two gates cascade into the decoder-conditioning (`cond`, model.py:179) and the aux in the same factor. Also note: both gates can only attenuate; the exemplar path is never given a mechanism to amplify.

## 2. Learning signature vs the sibling gate child

Data honesty: this node's local `run/` has only `run_node.log` — the tensorboard file that N0003_h0009 synced (and whose 32-point curve its qual.md cites) was **not** synced here. So the minimum lands on the **last epoch**, matching the sibling gate child's signature of a descent still moving at the schedule edge — but I cannot reproduce the sibling's monotonic 32/32 claim from this log.

The dominant qualitative gap is not in curve shape but in level: sibling 22.608 vs this node 23.382 (Δ ≈ +0.77, about two-thirds of the +1.15 channel-gate-only penalty and ~1.92 below the 21.459 parent). Where that extra ~0.8 plausibly comes from: (a) **two coupled moving targets** — the per-exemplar gate reads the exemplar tokens while the channel gate reads the fine GAP; both distributions are being trained simultaneously, so each gate's input drifts under the other's updates, and the effective token scale is a product of two independent bounded scalars the optimizer must coordinate; (b) **redundant/competing dof** — neither gate is supervised toward a target (no gate stats logged), so a whole manifold of (eg, g) pairs reproduces any net scale, and nothing prevents the 33K-channel gate (chosen for H0009's capacity) from cancelling or chasing the 257-param exemplar gate; (c) **init** — at 0.48× a 32-epoch cosine schedule has to relearn the condenser's operating point from further away than even the sibling's 0.5×; the small identity-preserving 0.953 of H0010 is swamped by the co-embedded 0.5 channel gate, so the carefully-chosen bias+3 init buys almost nothing in this joint run. (a)+(b) (interaction) rather than init alone best explains why this node beats neither the parent nor the sibling.

## 3. What the PAIR of ablations says about the exemplar path

H0009 alone (shared per-channel rescale) → −1.15. H0010+H0009 (per-template scalar composed with per-channel rescale) → −1.92. Two independent top-level tests and both **degrade by more than the 0.30 bar**. Neither run ever tested a pure per-exemplar gate or a pure identity-init-only perturbation, so no single-gate attribution is licensed, but the joint reading is coherent: the champion's exemplar tokens already sit near a local scale optimum, and ANY multiplicative reweighting of that path — shared-channel, per-template, or both — taxes it twice over, first as an init perturbation the head must re-buy under a tight budget, second as extra coupled parameters that can interfere with the encoder and the GCA aux. The fact that the richer, more faithful per-template mechanism (H0010, exactly the BECAUSE that H0009 claimed but could not express) made things **worse** rather than better on top of H0009 is the strongest available hint that exemplar-scale sensitivity, not mechanism mismatch, is the binding constraint on this path. A hardening hypothesis for the lab to note: gating the exemplar path may need identity-preserving init AND the removal of co-embedded scale perturbations to even approximate parity, and its rank-1 (per-image) factorization is too weak to test per-template-per-channel steering.
